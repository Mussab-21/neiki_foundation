Phase 1 ISLD Donated Water Projects & Solutions

Comprehensive Water Solutions

Water is the foundation of life. Our Phase 1 initiative focuses on delivering diverse, sustainable water systems tailored to the specific geographical and communal needs of underserved regions.

01

Family Water Hand Pump

Designed for individual households, providing safe and immediate access to clean drinking water right at home.

02

Community Water Hand Pump

Larger, more durable pumps installed in central locations to serve multiple families and small neighborhoods.

03

Deep Water Well

Excavated deep into the earth to reach pure aquifers, ensuring a reliable water supply even during dry seasons.

04

Solar Water Plant

Eco-friendly water extraction utilizing solar panels to pump water from deep reserves without electricity costs.

05

Water Purification Plant

Medium to large-scale RO plants that filter out contaminants, serving entire regions with WHO-standard clean water.

06

Natural Water Supply

Projects designed to channel and distribute water from natural springs and mountain streams safely to communities.
